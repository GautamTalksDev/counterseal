Account

Your data, and how to take it all away.

What Counterseal keeps about you

Your name, your passkey's public key, your encrypted vault, token and receipts key hashes, agent public keys, holds, your audit log, your receipts counters and openings and, if you turn on alerts, each device's push address. Never your face, fingerprint, readable keys or private keys. Privacy policy

Delete account

Erases your account, passkeys, vault, tokens, agent keys, receipts keys, holds, sessions and audit log immediately, and unlinks your public receipts (they stay in the log, but nothing on our side says they were yours). Agents using your tokens stop working. This cannot be undone.
